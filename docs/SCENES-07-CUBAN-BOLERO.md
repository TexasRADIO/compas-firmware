# Cuban bolero from trova to canción

## Two bars to play

Early Cuban bolero grew through singers, guitarists and oral circulation.
María Teresa Linares follows the move from eastern Cuba toward Havana, the
spread of records by trovadores, and the later arrival of these songs in
son sextet repertories. In her account, guitar accompaniment develops its
own recurring figures while the sung line can vary. She gives the example
of Eusebio Delfín's *Aquella boca*, heard both with his guitar and in the
Sexteto Habanero's bolero-son setting.
[Linares, El bolero en el Caribe, pp. 16–24](https://es.scribd.com/document/356557146/El-Bolero-en-El-Caribe-Surgimiento-en-Cuba-y-Su-Proceso-de-Cambios-Hasta-1930)

Linares describes two witnesses to *Bolero de Manzanillo*. The anonymous
version has the first cell below. The version edited by Anselmo López and
dated 1900, for voice and piano with Ramón Moreno's text, also uses the
cinquillo. INBAL catalogues a 1900 voice-and-piano witness under Moreno.
The catalogue gives the publisher as unspecified. Establishing whether
its copy is the same edition requires inspecting the title page and music.
[Linares, pp. 17–19](https://es.scribd.com/document/356557146/El-Bolero-en-El-Caribe-Surgimiento-en-Cuba-y-Su-Proceso-de-Cambios-Hasta-1930)
· [INBAL Archivo Lavalle–Kuri title catalogue](https://dblavallekuri.inba.gob.mx/xmlui/handle/123456789/8/browse?etal=-1&offset=69&order=ASC&rpp=20&sort_by=1&type=title)

**Bolero Trova** alternates the two cells once each. Compás supplies
the order, accents, tempo, plucked tone and root/fifth bass. The
pitched cues hold one tonal center until the player changes key.
The two cells come from Linares's descriptions. The song's melody,
lyrics, harmony and complete accompaniment remain outside the study.

Open **Cuban bolero** from the browser rehearsal hub. **Bolero Trova**
plays both cues and bass at 76 quarter notes per minute. **Play Your
Guitar** leaves only the bass. **Bolero Canción** keeps the quarter
pulse and changes to a two-bar 4/4 backing with bongó, maracas and
conga. **Play Your Bass** returns to the 2/4 guitar cue. Use PRESETS
to cue each slot while playing. On Band, Break in the full Trova slot
also leaves the bass. The complete Trova preset is number 27 from the
727 pads. The [727 conga bank](BOLERO-CONGA-BANK.md) holds separately
sourced hand-percussion patterns for later bolero settings.

## Tristeza and the recording trail

Pepe Sánchez's *Tristeza* or *Tristezas* is a starting point for the
Santiago de Cuba song tradition. Mexico's Fonoteca Nacional presents
Ábrego y Picazo's *Tristezas* as a Mexico City recording from 1907.
UCLA catalogues the duo's Columbia C436, matrix 5906, under *Un Beso*,
without a date on its item page. The Díaz-Ayala discography associates
that matrix with Sánchez and gives approximately 1907. The Columbia
C-series discography dates it to 1908–09. The earliest date and the
relationship between the surviving recording witnesses deserve separate
checks before anyone uses them to date the composition or a score.
[Fonoteca Nacional, FN08030064768_01](https://fonotecanacional.cultura.gob.mx/escucha/miscelanea-sonora/feria-del-libro-cuba)
· [UCLA, Columbia C436](https://frontera.library.ucla.edu/recordings/un-beso)
· [FIU, Díaz-Ayala IX Varios](https://latinpop.fiu.edu/IX%20Varios.pdf)
· [Columbia C-series matrix discography](https://recordingpioneers.com/docs/Columbia_C_Series.pdf)

Two notation leads are distinct. Córdoba García's 2021 thesis
identifies a melodic guide in the custody of Perucho Mejía at figure
21. The archive record is accessible and the thesis download
requires a login. The open *resDanza* reproduction on printed p. 36
shows a page of typeset voice and piano. The visible vocal pickup
begins after an instrumental opening. The page gives a source-site
credit and does not establish the original print date or a complete
edition. Compás lists both leads and the recording without including
a score, MIDI or sound recording for this song.
[Bellas Artes institutional repository](https://repository.bellasartes.edu.co/handle/123456789/7)
· [resDanza no. 5, pp. 36–37](https://uach.mx/assets/media/publications/2023/6/5866_resdanza/fa_resdanza_numero_5.pdf#page=36)

A later acquisition located the two-page Orovio witness. See the
[follow-up score notes](SCENES-08-MEXICAN-BOLERO.md#the-cuban-sources).
