# A tribute to Johnny Canales

Johnny Canales brought Tejano, conjunto and other regional Mexican acts to a
television audience across the United States and Mexico. Compás pays tribute
to that musical setting with its opening portrait and song-change cameo.

The portrait illustration uses a photograph for the likeness and the BB-BOB
character artwork for its ink and color treatment. The fixed portrait is
cleared for distribution with Compás under the release scope in
[LICENSING.md](../LICENSING.md).
