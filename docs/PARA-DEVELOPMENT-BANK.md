# Pará development arrangements

This is a private Compás development set, not a public genre pack. It turns
four previously isolated 727 auditions into complete, playable Compás
arrangements while retaining their evidence boundary. It does not replace an
existing regional study, assert that any entry represents all music from Pará,
or claim that the original instruments were TR-727 sounds.

| Study | Status | What the playable arrangement retains | What Compás authors |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Carimbó · Marapanim | Source-cell-based reduction | The credited two-bar curimbó and milheiro role relationship | Accent response and Break texture; no bass line |
| Belém · Tecnobrega | Source-cell-based electronic-drum proxy | The readable Bumbo/Caixa event relationship | Energy accents and a labelled tom transition; no keyboard or bass part |
| Pará Lambada · Quebradas | Authored lead-floor study | A documented role field: constant low drum, Caixa cells and fast tempo | Every programmed onset and live form; no automatic guitar or bass |
| Pará Guitarrada · lead-space | Authored lead-space study | The named *Lambada do Rei* tempo and ensemble/ostinato role map | Every drum onset and the performance floor; no source guitar or bass line |

## How to review it

After the real-bank browser build, open
[`Compas-Para-Review.html`](../build/Compas-Para-Review.html), or choose
**Pará · source-labelled arrangement studies** from Rehearsal sets. The same set
is retained as `repertoire/para-development-set.json` for Set Builder. Each
entry starts in Base. Try Lift, then Break, with a guitarist, singer or
percussionist playing over it. The absence of a programmed bass line is
deliberate where the retained evidence does not expose one.

The four patterns are compatible with the ordinary three Energy states, but
those states are machine performance arrangements—not evidence of historical
form. The 727 page remains available to edit or replace the percussion; save
those edits separately from the source ledger.

## Source and review boundary

The local research records used for this pass are held in the pattern-bank
ledger: `PAR-06` (Marapanim score), `PAR-07`/`PAR-08` (Belém tecnobrega
fieldwork and printed cell), and `PAR-03`/`PAR-04`/`PAR-10` (Mestre Vieira,
guitarrada and Pará lambada role research). The detailed source-versus-authored
notes travel with the integration handover. A Pará percussionist, guitarreiro,
arranger or producer must review the playable reductions before a release-bank
decision. A disagreement is evidence: it is recorded in the ledger, not
smoothed into a supposedly definitive preset.

No physical FM-1 write, release tag or public deployment is part of this
development branch.
