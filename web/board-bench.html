      On Band, press ARP and tap a part key for a fill into that part. ARP also works on its own for a fill.

      Rebajada: FX → Mix, KNOB4 SPEED slows the whole band and lowers its pitch. Turn to 100 for normal speed. Speed is a live master setting; saved shows keep the original tempo and reopen at 100%.

      Written songs: on Band, KNOB4 SHIFT transposes accompaniment and guide together. Zero is printed pitch; live changes wait for the next bar.

      La Adelita follows the 2016 arrangement by David Edward Kemp, CC BY 4.0. Compás renders the written pitches and rhythm with its score synth and makes Alto 1 an optional guide.

      La Valentina follows Stefan Apke’s 2019 guitar edition of Ponce’s arrangement. Compás uses its score synth and keeps the repeated form. The score-data adaptation uses CC BY-SA 4.0.

      Juana Quilina follows the 2011 melody engraving by Cheposo, CC BY-SA 3.0. The Compás score adaptation keeps the written pitches and rhythm, plays the nine bars on its score synth at an editable 108 BPM, and omits lyrics. The adapted score data uses the same license.
