# Compás 1.3

Compás turns the M-VAVE FM-1 into a bilingual Latin rhythm section: drums,
bass, hand percussion, a TR-727-inspired percussion instrument and the Hanan
Kumbia kit. Call an intro, verse, chorus, bridge, break or ending from the
keyboard and the band changes on the musical boundary.

Version 1.3 expands the bank to 38 source-labelled groove studies, adds 6/8
and 12/8 timing, gives Kumbia its own LEVEL / NIVEL control and restores
6.02 dB at the final digital output conversion. It retains Compás 1.2's ROOM,
tempo-synced ECHO, GLUE, MIDI clock, Sideman and two-way Big Beat Editor.
Both packages contain the same instrument and languages; they differ only in
the language shown at first boot.

Choose the English-default or Spanish-default package. The firmware identity
remains `FM-1_92210003`. Change the screen language at any time: open GLO and
turn ALGORITHM. The physical VOLUME control is the overall MASTER. On Kumbia,
K4 is LEVEL / NIVEL; OCT− and OCT+ change the sample CLOCK / RELOJ in Hz.

The 38 entries are accompaniment studies rather than claims to reproduce a
whole regional practice. Their source notes distinguish published reductions,
authored support, proxy voices and work still awaiting local musician review.
Compound-meter BPM counts dotted-quarter pulses; MIDI clock remains 24 PPQN.

Compás keeps shows and user rhythms in its CPS6 journal across power-off.
Installing firmware rewrites that journal, so export your show before an
update. Exact package hashes and qualification boundaries are in
[RELEASE.json](RELEASE.json). See [BUILDING.md](BUILDING.md) for reproduction,
[the English guide](docs/GUIDE-1.3-EN.md),
[the Spanish guide](docs/GUIDE-1.3-ES.md) and
